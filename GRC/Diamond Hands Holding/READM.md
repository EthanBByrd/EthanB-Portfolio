## Overview

This project portfolio represents a comprehensive enterprise-level **cybersecurity governance, risk, and security services design initiative** for Diamond Hands Holding Inc. (DHHI).

The work simulates real-world responsibilities of a cybersecurity consultant, GRC analyst, and security architect, focusing on building a scalable and structured cybersecurity program aligned with industry standards such as NIST CSF, NIST SP 800-series publications, and ISO/IEC 27001.

Across multiple deliverables, this project covers the full lifecycle of cybersecurity program development—from foundational research and framework selection to governance design, staffing strategy, and security service implementation. The result is a cohesive, audit-ready set of documents that reflect executive-level planning, operational alignment, and long-term cybersecurity maturity growth.

---

## Objective

To design and document a complete cybersecurity program framework for a simulated organization, ensuring alignment with industry standards and best practices.

The end goal is to transition the organization from a fragmented security posture to a mature, structured, and strategically governed cybersecurity environment.

## GRC Relevance

This project covers all three domains of GRC:

- **Governance**: Establishing leadership structures, policy frameworks, and accountability models  
- **Risk**: Applying structured methodologies to evaluate, manage, and reduce cybersecurity risks  
- **Compliance**: Aligning organizational practices with NIST standards and producing audit-ready documentation  

This makes the project an ideal portfolio piece for roles in cybersecurity governance, risk analysis, compliance auditing, and IT security consulting.
